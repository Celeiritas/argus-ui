# Leafy Realm theme – assets
Artwork (agent medallions, Medusa, Zeus, Poseidon, source images): © Celeiritas, all rights reserved. Not covered by the code's MIT license.

Sound effects are **not in this repository**. The author's copies came from sites such as myinstants.com, where clips are user-uploaded with no clear license, so they are not redistributed. See `sfx/README.md` for the file names and how to add your own.

Placeholders (4.48.0): `icons/chiron.png` is a plain lettered medallion (C) in the style of the old Heracles placeholder, made by a script, until the owner draws and approves its art. `icons/daedalus.png` was the lettered D until 4.54.1; it is now the owner's pick of 2026-11-17 (source `gods/daedalus.svg`). The retired agents' medallions (`chronos`, `hephaestus`, `poseidon`, `heracles`) stay in `icons/` and still ship, untouched; no agent uses them now.
